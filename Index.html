A gentle, private check-in to help you reflect on mood, stress, sleep and support. There are no right or wrong answers.

A quick note: This is a general wellness reflection tool, not a validated clinical screening test or a diagnosis. Your answers stay in this browser and are not uploaded or saved by this page.

Before you begin

Think about how you have felt over the past 2 weeks. Choose the answer that best reflects your experience. This usually takes about 2–3 minutes.

Need urgent support now? If you may harm yourself or someone else, or cannot stay safe, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department. In India, call 112 for emergencies or Tele-MANAS 14416 for mental health support.
